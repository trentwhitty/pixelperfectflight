About us

Born in SWFL. Built for local brands.

Trent Whitaker is a Southwest Florida native and marketing professional who studied Marketing at Florida Atlantic University. He has quickly established himself in the content creation and digital marketing space, helping over 300 local businesses, real estate professionals, and entrepreneurs elevate their brands through high-impact visual media and strategic online presence.

Beyond local businesses, Trent has also managed social media accounts for publicly traded companies and contributed to major product launches that generated over 500,000 units sold, giving him experience operating at both local and national marketing levels.

As an FAA Part 107 Certified Drone Pilot, Trent is licensed to capture aerial imagery wherever projects require, from luxury real estate and commercial developments to large-scale marketing campaigns.

Through his company, Trent blends cinematic video production, strategic marketing insight, and modern social media execution to help clients stand out in crowded markets. Whether producing high-end real estate media or building full digital campaigns, he approaches every project with precision, creativity, and a focus on results.
